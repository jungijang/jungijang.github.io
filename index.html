I am an Assistant Professor in the Graduate School of Data Science at Seoul National University, with a joint appointment at SNU College. Before joining SNU, I was an Assistant Professor at DGIST and a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I worked with Prof. Hanghang Tong. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Seoul National University, where I was advised by Prof. U Kang. I also received my B.S. in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering from Seoul National University, with a double major in Computer Science and Engineering. My research focuses on scalable data mining systems, model efficiency and compression, and applied AI. I have received best paper awards, including the Best Research Paper Award at KDD 2021 and the Best Paper Award Honorable Mention at ICDE 2022.
